! Title: Watchpost: base rules (all blocking levels)
! Description: Exceptions essential to a Galaxy phone (Play Services, Play Store, FCM push,
!   banking apps, updates) + Google/Samsung telemetry blocks, one per line, each one commented.
!   Loaded by AdGuard Home as a local list (ID 1), in addition to the HaGeZi lists.
! Homepage: infra/adguard/ in the Watchpost repository
!
! AdGuard syntax: "||domain^" blocks the domain and its subdomains;
! "@@||domain^$important" allows it, even if it appears in another list (HaGeZi, threats).
! The exceptions are deliberately limited to what really breaks the phone: each one also
! weakens threat filtering for that domain.
!
! ============================== EXCEPTIONS (do not block) ==============================
!
! --- Android connectivity check: blocked => "Connected, no Internet", switch to mobile data
@@||connectivitycheck.gstatic.com^$important
@@||connectivitycheck.android.com^$important
@@||clients3.google.com^$important
@@||clients4.google.com^$important
!
! --- Push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging): messaging apps, banking apps (3-D Secure
!     payment approval), alerts. mtalk = persistent FCM connection.
@@||mtalk.google.com^$important
@@||mtalk4.google.com^$important
@@/^alt[0-9]+-mtalk\.google\.com$/$important
@@||android.googleapis.com^$important
@@||fcm.googleapis.com^$important
@@||fcmtoken.googleapis.com^$important
! Firebase installation token: required to obtain an FCM push token since 2020.
@@||firebaseinstallations.googleapis.com^$important
!
! --- Play Store and Play Services: accounts, device registration, downloads,
!     Play Integrity (required by most banking apps).
@@||android.clients.google.com^$important
@@||play.googleapis.com^$important
@@||play-fe.googleapis.com^$important
@@||play-lh.googleusercontent.com^$important
@@||accounts.google.com^$important
@@||www.googleapis.com^$important
@@||gvt1.com^$important
@@||dl.google.com^$important
!
! --- Google system updates (Mainline modules, Play System Update)
@@||update.googleapis.com^$important
!
! --- Samsung software updates (FOTA). If an update fails, look at the AdGuard query log
!     (domains *.samsungdm.com, *.samsungapps.com blocked by HaGeZi).
@@||ospserver.net^$important
@@||samsungotn.net^$important
!
! ============================== TELEMETRY BLOCKS ==============================
!
! --- Google / Firebase: audience measurement and diagnostic logs of the apps
! Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase), built into most apps.
||app-measurement.com^
! Firebase logging (usage events sent by the Firebase SDK).
||firebaselogging.googleapis.com^
||firebaselogging-pa.googleapis.com^
! Crashlytics crash reports (contain model, memory state, installation identifiers).
||firebasecrashlytics.googleapis.com^
||crashlyticsreports-pa.googleapis.com^
||crashlytics.com^
! Google Play Services telemetry (usage and performance statistics).
||play-services-telemetry.googleapis.com^
! Client metrics of the Google apps (Chrome, WebView).
||clientmetrics-pa.googleapis.com^
! Google Analytics for the web and Tag Manager (web pages and app WebViews).
||google-analytics.com^
||googletagmanager.com^
!
! --- Google: advertising (advertising identifier, bidding, conversions)
||doubleclick.net^
||googlesyndication.com^
||googleadservices.com^
||adservice.google.com^
!
! --- Samsung: advertising and diagnostic collection
! Samsung ad network (ads in Samsung apps: Weather, Health, Themes, Galaxy Store).
||samsungads.com^
! "Device Intelligence" collection (usage diagnostic data).
||dc.di.atlas.samsung.com^
! Knox usage statistics (sent by the Knox components, outside enterprise management).
||analytics.samsungknox.com^
! Game Optimizing Service (GOS) telemetry: game profiles and usage.
||gos-gsp.io^
